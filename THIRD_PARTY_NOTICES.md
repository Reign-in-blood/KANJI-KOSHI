# Third-party data notices

## OpenJLPT

KANJI KŌSHI includes a pinned snapshot of kanji data from OpenJLPT.

- Project: OpenJLPT
- Repository: https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT
- Revision: `c42fd9fa3777bfc1775446f7c418d549dfd6e4cf`
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

OpenJLPT documents these upstream sources for the kanji dataset:

- KANJIDIC2 / Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG): kanji readings, meanings, stroke counts, grade and frequency.
- Jonathan Waller's JLPT resources (Tanos): N5-N1 level assignments.

The original OpenJLPT notice and license are stored under `data/upstream/openjlpt/`.

## KANJIDIC2 French meanings

The machine-readable datasets under `data/upstream/` and the derived `data/generated/kanji.json` are treated as CC BY-SA 4.0 data. Data licensing is documented separately from the application's source code.
